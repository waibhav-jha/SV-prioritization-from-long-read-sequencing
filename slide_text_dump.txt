Slide 02:
  PROBLEM CONTEXT
  Clinical Problem: The Structural Variant Interpretation Bottleneck
  High-throughput variant discovery creates an unsustainable manual curation bottleneck
  >20,000
  SVs Per Genome
  Structural variants (>=50 bp) account for the greatest number of divergent base pairs in human genomes and drive rare Mendelian disorders.
  40.0%
  Unresolved VUS
  40,741 variants in ClinVar have 'Uncertain Significance' (VUS) tags, leaving patients and geneticists with inconclusive clinical reports.
  Days / Case
  Manual Curation
  Standard clinical evaluation requires labor-intensive manual guideline cross-referencing, stalling diagnostic throughput in rare disease.
  CORE PROBLEM FORMULATION
  While 3rd-generation sequencing detects tens of thousands of structural variants with high precision, clinical laboratories lack automated, calibrated methods to rank high-risk variants and triage the 40% VUS bottleneck. An explainable AI prioritization framework is critically needed.

Slide 03:
  TECHNOLOGY DRIVERS
  Motivation: Long-Read Sequencing & Explainable AI
  Leveraging single-molecule long reads and interpretable machine learning
  THE POWER OF LONG-READ SEQUENCING
  Why PacBio HiFi & Oxford Nanopore (>10 kb)?
  •  Spanning Complex Repeats:
  Long reads cross repetitive regions and segmental duplications inaccessible to short-read Illumina.
  •  Base-Pair Breakpoint Precision:
  Callers (Sniffles2, cuteSV) resolve exact SV boundaries at near single-nucleotide resolution.
  •  Comprehensive Variant Discovery:
  Captures full spectrum: balanced inversions, complex translocations, and large insertions.
  THE EXPLAINABLE AI APPROACH
  Transparent, Calibrated Risk Prioritization
  •  Continuous Prioritization Index:
  Outputs a calibrated probability score (0.00 to 1.00) rather than rigid binary heuristic categories.
  •  Local TreeSHAP Attribution:
  Explains every single prediction by computing Shapley values for population AF, constraint, and length.
  •  Clinical Actionability Triage:
  Ranks candidate patient variants into 4 intuitive tiers (Tier 1 High to Tier 4 Low Risk).

Slide 05:
  RESEARCH CHALLENGES
  Research Gap: Critical Limitations in Current Approaches
  Four major scientific and methodological roadblocks identified in current practice
  01
  The 40% VUS Dilemma
  40.0% of clinical SVs remain classified as Variants of Uncertain Significance. Current pipelines lack continuous statistical scoring to rank and triage these ambiguous variants.
  02
  Severe Metadata Leakage
  Published ML models often include post-diagnostic clinical metadata (ReviewStatus stars, submitter counts, disease strings), achieving inflated, non-generalizable accuracy.
  03
  Genomic Locus Leakage
  Standard random train/test splits cause recurring CNV hotspots (e.g. 22q11.2, 16p11.2) to contaminate test sets, masking true generalization failure.
  04
  Black-Box Diagnostic Opacity
  Existing classifiers output opaque scores without explaining the exact biological mechanisms (gene constraint vs population rarity) driving the prediction.

  Structural Variant Types & Structural Proportions
  Deletions dominate clinical submissions and show strong pathogenic enrichment
  Deletions & Copy Number Loss (58.1%)
  •  45,862 Deletions + 23,892 Copy Number Loss.
  •  High clinical severity driven by gene haploinsufficiency and complete coding sequence loss.
  Duplications & Copy Number Gain (40.7%)
  •  21,798 Copy Number Gain + 8,735 Duplications.
  •  More frequently tolerated as benign polymorphic variation across healthy human populations.
  KEY OBSERVATION: 4.8x PATHOGENIC ODDS
  Deletions exhibit 4.8x higher pathogenic odds compared to duplications of equivalent genomic size, making structural variant type a critical biological feature in our ML architecture.

  1. Focal Coding Disruptions (1 kb – 10 kb):

  2. Megabase Chromosomal Syndromes (>1 Mb):

  Reported benign SVs cluster in non-coding polymorphic copy number segments.

Slide 11:
  POPULATION GENETICS
  Population Allele Frequencies & Purifying Selection
  gnomAD SV v2.1 baseline reveals extreme purifying selection on functional SVs
  ALLELE FREQUENCY SPECTRUM (N = 387,477)
  •  90.12% (349,202 SVs) are rare (AF < 1%) in healthy genomes.
  •  39.39% (152,640 SVs) are ultra-rare singletons (AF <= 0.0046%).
  •  Only 9.88% (38,275 SVs) are common benign polymorphisms (AF >= 1%).
  PURIFYING SELECTION ON FUNCTIONAL SVs
  •  Loss-of-Function (LoF) SVs: 91.8% are ultra-rare (AF < 0.1%).
  •  Promoter Overlaps: 84.3% are ultra-rare (AF < 0.1%).
  •  Intergenic SVs: Only 62.1% are ultra-rare (frequently polymorphic).
  Takeaway: High population frequency (AF > 1%) serves as a highly robust negative predictor of pathogenicity.

Slide 12:
  STATISTICAL VALIDATION
  Statistical Hypothesis Testing: Biological Differences
  Formal pre-training statistical validation of size and variant type associations
  HYPOTHESIS 1: SV LENGTH VS. PATHOGENICITY
  Mann-Whitney U Non-Parametric Test
  Test Parameters & Quantiles:
  •  Pathogenic Median: 51,100 bp (IQR: 2.9 kb – 1.58 Mb)
  •  Benign Median: 153,420 bp (IQR: 56.2 kb – 401.2 kb)
  •  Test Statistic: U = 2.69 x 10^8
  STATISTICAL RESULT
  p = 3.04 x 10^-77
  Statistically Significant (p < 10^-5). Proves pathogenic and benign SVs follow distinct genomic length regimes.
  HYPOTHESIS 2: SV TYPE VS. PATHOGENICITY
  Pearson's Chi-Square Contingency Test
  Contingency Breakdown:
  •  Deletions: 23,781 Pathogenic vs 624 Benign (4.8x odds)
  •  CN Gains: 5,479 Pathogenic vs 7,579 Benign (Benign enriched)
  •  Degrees of Freedom (dof): 11
  STATISTICAL RESULT
  Chi-Sq = 15,769.58 (p = 0.0000)
  Statistically Significant (p < 10^-300). Confirms non-random biological dependency between SV type and outcome.

Slide 13:
  DATA INTEGRATION
  Cross-Dataset Interval Matching & Harmonization
  Resolving coordinate discrepancies and fuzzy breakpoints across databases
  WHY EXACT ID MATCHING FAILS (0.0% OVERLAP)
  •  Exact Identifier Match: 0.0% overlap (Disjoint accession schemes).
  •  Strict Breakpoint Window (<= 100 bp): Only 0.38% match due to legacy array coordinate fuzziness (+/- 50 to 500 bp).
  •  Conclusion: Interval overlap matching is mathematically required.
  HIERARCHICAL OVERLAP PROTOCOL
  1. Standardize all coordinates to GRCh38 via UCSC LiftOver.
  2. Require strict SV Type concordance (DEL <-> DEL, DUP <-> DUP).
  3. Large CNVs (> 5 kb): >= 70% reciprocal overlap.
  4. Moderate SVs (500 bp – 5 kb): >= 50% reciprocal overlap.
  5. Unobserved ClinVar variants assigned AF = 0.0 (high rarity).

  CURRENT BIOLOGICAL & METHODOLOGICAL UNDERSTANDING
  Structural variant pathogenicity is strictly driven by structural category (Deletions > Duplications), size-dependent coding disruption, extreme population rarity (AF < 0.1%), and gene constraint (pLI / LOEUF). To prevent artificial model memorization, post-diagnostic metadata must be strictly excluded and evaluation must be performed across disjoint chromosomes.
